Privacy Policy

XO acts for you, so it needs some of your data. This page says exactly what XO collects, what it does with it, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

The short version. XO uses your data only to do the work you ask for. We don't sell it, we don't use it for ads, and we don't use it to train AI models. You can see, export or delete it at any time.

Who we are

XO is built by XOVU ("we", "us"). Our company registration in India is in progress; we will add our registered name and address here once it is complete. For anything about privacy, write to founder@xovu.ai.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and the ID of the account you sign in with.
  • Calendar data, when you connect Google Calendar: your events (titles, times, places, descriptions, attendees) and your free and busy times.
  • Email you ask XO to send, if you turn on sending: the messages you approve, their recipients, and the delivery result. XO does not read your inbox.
  • What you tell XO: your requests, your approvals and skips, and a history of the actions XO took for you.
  • App and device data: app version, device type, crash reports and notification tokens, so the app works and we can fix errors.
  • Early access requests: the name, email, role and company you send us.

How we use it

  • To suggest, prepare and carry out the actions you approve, and to check that they worked.
  • To keep a history of what XO did, so you can review it.
  • To keep XO secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account and XO's service.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.

XO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google data only to provide and improve the features you see in XO.
  • We don't transfer it to others except as needed to run those features (see "Who else processes your data"), for security, or to comply with law.
  • We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell it.
  • We don't use it to train generalised AI or machine learning models.
  • No person at XOVU reads it unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it's needed for security or to investigate abuse, or the law requires it.

You can remove XO's access at any time from your Google account permissions or from XO's settings. XO then stops reading your Google data and deletes what it holds under "How long we keep data".

Where your data is stored

XO stores your data in India. Some providers above process parts of it outside India to do their job; we only use providers that protect it under contract.

How long we keep data

  • We keep your data while your account is open.
  • If you delete your account or ask us to, we delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • We may keep a minimal record of actions XO took for you for longer only where the law requires it, and we'll tell you if so.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask what data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you can't.
  • Raise a complaint with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

To do any of this, write to founder@xovu.ai. We reply within 7 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the keys XO uses to reach your accounts are stored encrypted, separately from other data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

XO is for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in XO or by email before the change applies.
