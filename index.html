How it works

Ask, approve, done.

1. Ask

"Find 30 minutes with Priya on Thursday afternoon." XO looks at your calendar and suggests a time that works.

2. Approve

XO shows you exactly what it will do. Approve it, change it, or skip it.

3. Done

XO makes the change in Google Calendar and tells you once it's confirmed.

XOExample

Move Thursday's product sync with Priya to 4:30–5:00 pm?

Done Product sync is now Thu 23 Oct, 4:30–5:00 pm.

Example only. Names and times are made up.

Your data

Private by default.

Your data is stored in India, never sold, never used for ads, and never used to train AI models. Delete it whenever you like.

How XO uses your Google account

When you connect Google Calendar, XO reads your events and free time so it can suggest times, and creates, moves or cancels events only after you approve. Read how XO handles Google data.
