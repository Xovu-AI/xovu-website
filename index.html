How it works

From request to finished.

1. Ask

"Get the team offsite booked for 12 people in March." XO understands the goal and starts on it.

2. Work

XO handles each step: it finds options, fills in details and follows up until the task is complete.

3. Done

XO tells you once the task is finished, with what it did.

XOExample

Book a table for six near the office on Thursday at 7 pm?

Example only. Names and times are made up.
